Cancellation Policy:
Customers can cancel an order within 5 minutes of placing it without any charge. Once the restaurant kitchen accepts or starts preparing the order, cancellations are strictly not permitted and no refund will be issued.

Refund Policy:
If payment was debited but the order failed, the refund reflects in the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days automatically. For food quality issues, complaints must be submitted within 2 hours.

Delivery Guarantee:
Standard delivery is 30 to 45 minutes within 5 km. If delivery exceeds 45 minutes due to restaurant operational delays, the customer is eligible for a 20% courtesy discount code.

Operational Hours:
The kitchen operates from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily. Queries outside these hours are queued for next-day resolution.
